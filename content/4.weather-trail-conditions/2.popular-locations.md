## Glacier National Park, Montana

Waterton's cross-border neighbour. The park is big enough that the west side (Lake McDonald)
and the east side (Many Glacier, St. Mary, Two Medicine) regularly get completely different
weather on the same day, so check the gateway town nearest your trailhead rather than the park
as a whole. Environment Canada does not cover the US side — the NWS point forecast is the
equivalent.
