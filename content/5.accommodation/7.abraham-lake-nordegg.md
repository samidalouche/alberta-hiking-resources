**David Thompson Country (Nordegg / Abraham Lake)** — Alberta Parks

- **Reservable** — Fish Lake / Shunda Lake recreation area (book via [Alberta Parks](https://albertaparks.ca/reserve/)); [Crescent Falls](https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/central/crescent-falls-pra/information-facilities/camping/crescent-falls/) *(also has FCFS sites)*

**Icefields Parkway — Banff National Park** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/camping)

- **Reservable** — Rampart Creek, Silverhorn Creek
- **First-come, first-served** — Mosquito Creek, Waterfowl Lakes

North of the Columbia Icefield the Parkway enters Jasper National Park — see [Jasper, Mount Robson, and Valemount](/accommodation/jasper-mount-robson-valemount).

- **Hostels** — [HI Nordegg / Shunda Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-nordegg); along the Icefields Parkway: [HI Mosquito Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-mosquito-creek), [HI Rampart Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-rampart-creek), and [HI Hilda Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-hilda-creek) (group rental only, 8 km south of the Icefields Centre)
